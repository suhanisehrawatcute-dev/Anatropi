A distrubingly short time ago, in a land uncomfortably close by...
Anatropi
An orphan named Elera was born in a small town. Her parents had died in a raid done by the king's army. She had no one to take care of her, so she was left to fend for herself. Despite living on the streets, she was a compassionate and kind person. She always felt she was destined for more than what she was currently living. She wanted to make a difference in the world, she wanted so no child would have to go through what she had gone through. She wanted to be a hero, but she knew that she was powerless.
Until one fateful day...
